01 About

I'm a data analyst and aspiring data scientist based in Memphis, TN, currently pursuing an MS in Data Science at the University of Memphis (3.9 GPA) as a Weil Graduate Fellow. My work spans SQL and statistical analysis, machine learning model evaluation, BI dashboarding, and data-quality auditing — most recently applied to real caseload and operational data as a Data Analyst Intern at the Shelby County District Attorney General's office.

Before graduate school, I worked as a data analyst at TCS and in a data analytics internship at Pantech in India, and I currently also serve as a Teaching Assistant for Machine Learning at the University of Memphis and as an IT Technical Assistant on the university's ITS Service Desk. Every project below is built from real coursework, internships, or self-directed practice — the results are what I actually measured, not what would look good.
