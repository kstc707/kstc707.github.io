about A little about me

Right now I'm an MS Data Science candidate at the University of Memphis (3.9 GPA, Weil Graduate Fellow), and I spend my days split between two very different data jobs: as a Data Analyst Intern at the Shelby County District Attorney General's office, where I work with real caseload and operational data, and as a Teaching Assistant for Machine Learning, where I get to see the same concepts click for other people.

Before grad school I was a data analyst at TCS and did a data analytics internship at Pantech, back in India — and somewhere in the middle of all that I also kept the University's ITS Service Desk running as an IT Technical Assistant. If there's a thread through all of it, it's that I'd rather build the thing and see if it actually works than just read about how it's supposed to work. Every project below is something I actually ran, with the real numbers it actually produced — not the numbers that would look good.
